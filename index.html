About

Content that works isn't accidental. It's planned, briefed, shot, and read.

I'm a Social Media Marketing Executive with 1.5 years of experience managing social media for brands across genuinely different worlds: Men's Ethnic Wear, HVAC, Real Estate, Bags, and Cookware. Each industry taught me a different way a brand needs to sound.

Day to day, that means building monthly content calendars, digging into trends and competitor moves, and turning a brand goal into an actual content strategy. I coordinate with clients to align on creative direction, brief shoots with shot lists and mood boards, and handle everything from shoot execution to final edits. Then I track performance and spot opportunities for the next sprint.
